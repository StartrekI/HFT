Section 1

Understanding HFT

High-Frequency Trading is algorithmic trading where computers execute thousands to millions of trades per second.

Key Features of HFT

01
Ultra-low Latency
Execution in microseconds
02
Automated Systems
No manual intervention
03
Statistical Models
Math-driven decisions
04
Co-located Servers
Inside exchange data centers

Trading Types

Algorithmic sec-min
Quantitative ms-hours
HFT μs

Core Concepts

Market Microstructure

How trades happen inside exchanges

  • Order book dynamics
  • Liquidity analysis
  • Bid-ask spread
  • Market impact

Order Book

Real-time buy/sell orders

Bid100 @ $10.00
Ask50 @ $10.01

Latency

Signal to execution delay

In HFT, nanoseconds matter. Faster infrastructure directly translates to profit.

Arbitrage

Profit from price differences

Buy on cheaper exchange, sell on expensive one. Must execute before prices converge.

Common HFT Strategies

Market Making

Provide liquidity by quoting bid/ask prices. Profit from spread capture.

Bid: 100.00 | Ask: 100.01
Spread: $0.01
Statistical Arbitrage

Exploit statistical relationships between correlated instruments.

If A↑ and B stays flat
→ Short A, Long B
Latency Arbitrage

Exploit slower participants or cross-exchange delays.

Futures move → ETF follows
Trade before adjustment
